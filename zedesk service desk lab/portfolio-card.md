# Portfolio Summary — Zendesk Service Desk Lab

**Area:** IT Service Management / Service Desk Operations  
**Tool:** Zendesk Support, Zendesk Knowledge / Help Center  
**Environment:** Personal Zendesk trial, two test identities, Chrome regular and Incognito sessions  
**Completed:** October 22, 2026

Built a small service desk training environment to explore Zendesk from both an agent and customer perspective. Inspected ticket fields, status controls, priority and assignment, Help Center access, and requester interaction history. Activated the customer Help Center and used a separate fictional employee account (**Emily**) to submit a login-support request (**ticket #4**). Located the initially unassigned request from the agent side and assigned it to myself.

**Key skills:** Ticket intake · initial triage · ticket ownership · customer vs. agent experience · Help Center setup · ticket queue / search · ITSM fundamentals · technical documentation

**Scope:** Verified ticket creation and ownership workflow. Does not claim a production service desk deployment, an actual authentication fix, configured SLAs, or completed end-to-end incident resolution.
